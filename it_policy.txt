IT Security Policy - TechCorp Internal
Version 2.4, Updated January 2024

VPN Policy:
All employees must use corporate VPN when accessing company resources from home.
VPN client: Cisco AnyConnect. Server: vpn.techcorp.com
To reset VPN credentials: contact helpdesk@techcorp.com or call ext 4321.
VPN sessions timeout after 8 hours of inactivity.

Password Policy:
Passwords must be minimum 12 characters, include uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol.
Passwords expire every 90 days. Cannot reuse last 10 passwords.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all cloud service access.

Data Classification:
- Public: Marketing materials, job postings
- Internal: Project documents, meeting notes
- Confidential: Customer data, financial records, source code
Customer data must NEVER be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage.

Cloud Resource Access:
To request AWS access: submit ticket in ServiceNow with manager approval.
AWS access provisioned within 2 business days after approval.
All AWS access reviewed quarterly. Unused access revoked automatically after 30 days.

Leave Policy:
Annual leave: 20 days per year, accrued monthly.
Sick leave: 10 days per year, no carryover.
Maternity leave: 26 weeks fully paid.
Paternity leave: 4 weeks fully paid.
Leave requests must be submitted in HR portal minimum 2 weeks in advance for planned leave.
