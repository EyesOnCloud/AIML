IT Security Policy - TechCorp Internal
Version 3.0, Updated June 2026 - SUPERSEDES Version 2.4

VPN Policy:
All employees must use corporate VPN when accessing company resources from home.
VPN client: Cisco AnyConnect. Server: vpn.techcorp.com
To reset VPN credentials: contact helpdesk@techcorp.com or call ext 4321.
VPN sessions timeout after 8 hours of inactivity.

Password Policy:
Passwords must be minimum 12 characters, include uppercase, lowercase, number, and symbol.
Passwords expire every 90 days. Cannot reuse last 10 passwords.

Leave Policy (REVISED June 2026):
Annual leave: 22 days per year, accrued monthly.
Sick leave: 15 days per year, no carryover.
Maternity leave: 26 weeks fully paid.
Paternity leave: 6 weeks fully paid.
Leave requests must be submitted in HR portal minimum 2 weeks in advance for planned leave.
